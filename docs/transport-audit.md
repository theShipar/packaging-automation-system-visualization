# LineFlow transport audit (Build Kit doc 22, milestone M0)

Scope: product transport and station handover in the production scene. Engine conservation, KPIs and pages are out of scope except where transport touches them. Audited against `LineFlow_Build_Kit/docs/22-P0-SEAMLESS-TRANSPORT-AND-HANDOVER.md` and the reference image `LineFlow_Build_Kit/assets/lineflow-reference.png`.

## Spec conflicts and resolutions

1. **Camera direction.** The Hairline figures were drawn for az 45 (viewer at +x,+y). The reference image requires the line to rise to the right, which means viewing from the upstream end. Resolution: az -45 for the whole scene; figure paint orders were reviewed for the new viewer and moving parts that must interleave with products (scanner heads, hood posts, rails, robot forearm and gripper, carton walls, gate posts) moved into explicit front groups with depth keys.
2. **Engine stays geometry-light.** Doc 22 puts poses in the transport layer, but a transfer may only start when the head unit has physically reached the out port. Resolution: the domain owns a 1-D conveyor model (`src/domain/conveyor.ts`: belt speed, slot pitch, per-station infeed and exit lengths). Positions are scalar distances along a region, advanced by virtual time only. `src/transport/layout.ts` derives the same lengths from the figure ports and `tests/geometry.test.ts` asserts they match.
3. **Transfer duration.** Station transfer time stays 0.3 s (doc 07). The connector transfer lane is sized to belt speed × 0.3 s = 24 units, so default transfers run at belt speed. If an engineer changes `transferSeconds`, the same lane is crossed at `24 / transferSeconds`.
4. **Maintenance lock vs fault.** Doc 22 §6.1 forbids starting a transfer into a station in maintenance; §10.2 says a faulted station's infeed still accepts. Resolution: transfers are refused only while the receiver has a fault in `repairInProgress`.
5. **Reservation timing.** The engine reserves the receiving slot at the moment it starts the transfer (capacity and readiness are both checked at that instant), so `TRANSFER_RESERVED` and `TRANSFER_STARTED` share a timestamp and a reservation can never be lost before departure. `TRANSFER_CANCELLED` is defined but not emitted by this model.
6. **Buffer capacity vs drawn slots.** Doc 07 buffers hold 20-40 units; the drawn belts hold 1-4. Resolution: each region draws its visible slots exactly. Infeed shows the oldest units nearest the fixture; overflow waits at the infeed mouth. Outfeed shows the newest units leaving the fixture; overflow is the accumulation at the out port. Overflow is counted in a `+N` badge at the connector. Units only appear or disappear at those mouths, the intake feeder, inside a shipping case, and at the exit lane, never at a fixture.
7. **Robot buffers.** Robot `inQ` is the pickup belt; robot `outQ` is the deposit belt starting at the placement port. The deposit belt was lengthened to hold one visible slot past the placement port.
8. **QA timing.** The divert and reject chute start after `STATION_CYCLE_COMPLETED` and `QA_INSPECTION_REJECTED`; the tray shows the two latest rejects and the count.
9. **Engine version.** Unit tracks, transfer ids and wake-up events change the session shape, so `ENGINE_VERSION` moves to `lf-engine-1.1.0` and older saved sessions are not restored.
